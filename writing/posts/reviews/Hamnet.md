_Hamnet_ follows Shakespeare’s family as they navigate the death of his son. It is a lovely book about motherhood and grief. When we read about past plagues, it’s easy to assume people grew numb to death. Maggie O'Farrell refuses us that comfort. The events are almost unbearably sad, but the narrative is too clever to rely on cheap darkness and pity.
